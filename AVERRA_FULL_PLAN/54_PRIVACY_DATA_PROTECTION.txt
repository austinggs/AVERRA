PURPOSE
Defines privacy-by-design, data minimization, retention, rights, and third-party processing boundaries.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

MINIMIZATION
Collect only data necessary for identity, service delivery, security/fraud, finance, support, lawful reporting, paid entitlements, or donations. MiniPay destination verification should retain only the minimum destination/evidence data needed to establish operational control. Do not collect a full KYC identity dataset when formal KYC is not part of the approved product model.

PURPOSE MAPPING
Each data category has documented purpose, legal basis/consent requirements where applicable, access policy, retention, and deletion/anonymization handling.

THIRD PARTIES
Provider, analytics, communications, KYC, and payment providers are catalogued with processing purpose and contract/compliance status.

SENSITIVE DATA
KYC and financial information receive restricted access and enhanced retention/security controls.

USER RIGHTS
Support lawful requests for access, correction, deletion/restriction/objection where applicable, while preserving records that must lawfully be retained.

DAIMO
Record only integration data required to create/track/verify a payment session and reconcile settlement; do not collect provider data beyond purpose.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
This document is a planning/source-of-truth artifact. Concrete library choices, exact endpoint names, provider-specific payload formats, and deployment values may be finalized during implementation only when they preserve the stated invariants and pass the defined verification gates.

REVIEWS / COMMUNITY PRIVACY
Reviews are public user-generated content. Provide a clear notice that text, rating, and selected media may be visible to other users. Do not disclose private financial/support evidence merely because it is associated with a review. Collect and retain only data needed for publication, moderation, security, support, and applicable legal obligations. Image metadata should be minimized where practical.
