PURPOSE
Defines privileged game-management operations and safeguards.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

CONTROLS
Content enable/disable, event management, balance configuration, asset activation, leaderboard moderation, player investigation, and approved corrections.

SEGREGATION
Game admin privileges are distinct from financial payout privileges where practical.

AUDIT
Every privileged action records actor, reason, timestamp, target, before/after, source IP/session context where permitted, and ticket/reference.

SAFE OPERATIONS
Destructive actions are avoided; corrections should be compensating or reversible when technically possible.

EMERGENCY
Global or subsystem kill switches may disable reward claims or game actions without deleting history.
