PURPOSE
Defines survey discovery, qualification, completion, provider verification, and reward handling.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

QUALIFICATION
Survey qualification is not completion and must have its own status.

LIFECYCLE
available → started → qualifying → qualified/ineligible → completed → provider_verified → pending reward → available/reversed.

TRACKING
Record provider, survey ID, respondent/session ID, start/finish timestamps, qualification outcome, callback evidence, and reward state.

ABUSE
Detect duplicate participation, speed anomalies, device/account overlap, answer-quality signals, and provider-reported fraud flags.

USER MESSAGING
Explain that qualification can fail and that final reward status depends on provider verification.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
This document is a planning/source-of-truth artifact. Concrete library choices, exact endpoint names, provider-specific payload formats, and deployment values may be finalized during implementation only when they preserve the stated invariants and pass the defined verification gates.
