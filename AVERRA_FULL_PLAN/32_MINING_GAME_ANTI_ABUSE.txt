PURPOSE
Defines specialized anti-automation and game manipulation controls.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

THREATS
Botting, macro automation, impossible action rates, client memory manipulation, replay, multi-account farming, emulator/device patterns, and leaderboard manipulation.

SIGNALS
Action timing, device/session patterns, request anomalies, state/version mismatches, impossible progression, and cross-system fraud signals.

RESPONSE
Low-risk friction, rate limiting, temporary hold, leaderboard exclusion, reward hold, manual review, or account enforcement according to policy.

FINANCIAL BOUNDARY
Suspicion can hold a reward or trigger review, but fraud controls do not silently mutate the financial ledger.

PRIVACY
Collect only signals necessary for risk purposes and apply retention/access controls.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
