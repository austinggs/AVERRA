PURPOSE
Operational matrix for deciding whether a provider may be integrated or used in production.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

REQUIRED COLUMNS
Provider, type, Nigeria availability, incentive compatibility, API/postback support, signature/auth mechanism, conversion states, reversal support, settlement terms, payout currency/rail, commercial approval, compliance review, integration owner, status, last verification date.

DECISION GATES
A provider cannot be marked live without successful technical integration tests, callback authenticity validation, duplicate/replay tests, economic validation, and business/compliance approval.

DYNAMIC NATURE
Matrix entries expire and must be revalidated. “Known to exist” is not equivalent to “currently approved for Averra.”

TESTING RULE
Testing must use sandbox/provider-approved mechanisms or controlled non-production traffic. Owner self-completion of live incentivized offers is prohibited.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
This document is a planning/source-of-truth artifact. Concrete library choices, exact endpoint names, provider-specific payload formats, and deployment values may be finalized during implementation only when they preserve the stated invariants and pass the defined verification gates.
