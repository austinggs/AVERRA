PURPOSE
Defines product, financial, advertising, provider, operational, and game analytics.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

DOMAINS
Product funnel, earning activity, provider performance, financial/reconciliation, payouts, fraud, advertising, support, and Mining Game.

SOURCE HIERARCHY
Analytics consumes authoritative events; it never becomes the source of financial or gameplay truth.

FINANCIAL DASHBOARDS
Provider receivables, settled revenue, earned reward liability, User Funding Balance activity, withdrawals, payment completion, deposits awaiting confirmation, fee revenue, reserves, reconciliation variance.

OPERATIONAL DASHBOARDS
Provider uptime, callback failures, queue depth, payout aging, Hurry requests, expired Daimo sessions, support backlog, fraud review queue.

PRIVACY
Use aggregation/pseudonymization where possible and restrict access to sensitive analytics.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
This document is a planning/source-of-truth artifact. Concrete library choices, exact endpoint names, provider-specific payload formats, and deployment values may be finalized during implementation only when they preserve the stated invariants and pass the defined verification gates.

RELATED DOCUMENTS
See 71_ARCHITECTURAL_LAWS.md, 82_SOURCE_INDEX.md, and the domain-specific database/API/acceptance documents for cross-system contracts.

REVIEWS / COMMUNITY ANALYTICS
Track review volume, rating distribution, verified-review ratio, comment/reply volume, report rate, moderation queue age, media upload failure rate, and notification creation/delivery health. Analytics must not be used to fabricate ratings or suppress negative feedback and are not financial truth.
