PURPOSE
Defines advertiser onboarding, campaigns, tasks, targeting, conversion verification, budgets, and billing.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

HIERARCHY
Advertiser → Campaign → Task/Offer → Targeting → User exposure → Conversion → Verification → Billing/reporting.

CAMPAIGN CONTROLS
Budget, objective, geography, demographics where lawful, device, schedule, frequency, reward rule, verification method, and status.

FUNDING
Campaign budgets must support promised rewards and platform fees. Budget reservation prevents overspend.

VERIFICATION
Conversion tracking uses server-side events/provider callbacks where possible; client reporting is evidence only.

BILLING
Advertiser charges reconcile to verified conversions and agreed pricing, not raw clicks unless the campaign is explicitly CPC/CPM and compliant.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
This document is a planning/source-of-truth artifact. Concrete library choices, exact endpoint names, provider-specific payload formats, and deployment values may be finalized during implementation only when they preserve the stated invariants and pass the defined verification gates.
