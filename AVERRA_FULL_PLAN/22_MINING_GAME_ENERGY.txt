PURPOSE
Defines energy generation, consumption, caps, regeneration, and anti-cheat rules.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

ENERGY MODEL
Current energy, maximum capacity, regeneration rate, last-calculation timestamp, modifiers, and temporary boosts.

SERVER AUTHORITY
Energy is computed from server time and authoritative modifiers; client countdowns are presentation-only.

CONSUMPTION
Actions declare energy cost. The server validates sufficient energy atomically with the action.

ABUSE CONTROLS
Client clock changes, replayed consumption requests, impossible regeneration, and duplicate action IDs are rejected or flagged.

PRODUCT POLICY
Energy is game state, not money.
