PURPOSE
Defines upgrade trees, costs, prerequisites, and application.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

UPGRADE DEFINITION
Target entity/type, level, cost, time, prerequisites, modifiers, unlocks, version, and active dates.

APPLICATION
Upgrade requests are checked and committed server-side in a single authoritative state transition.

ROLLBACK
Failed upgrades are rejected; successful upgrades are historical game events. Administrative corrections are compensating events.

BALANCE
Upgrade economy is configurable and separately testable to avoid accidental linkage to financial values.

CONTENT VERSIONING
Balance changes must be versioned and auditable.
