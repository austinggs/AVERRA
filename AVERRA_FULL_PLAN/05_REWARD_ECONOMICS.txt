PURPOSE
Defines how rewards are calculated, funded, held, and reversed.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

REWARD SOURCE
Every financial reward has exactly one traceable primary funding source: verified provider economics, advertiser budget, or approved Averra-funded promotional budget.

REWARD LIFECYCLE
ELIGIBILITY_CHECKED → PENDING → APPROVED/AVAILABLE → WITHDRAWN or REVERSED/CHARGEBACK, with holds and review states as needed.

NOTE: the line above is a narrative description of the flow. The normative enum is
the reward_state type specified in doc 35 (ELIGIBLE, PENDING, AVAILABLE, ON_HOLD,
REVERSED, CHARGEBACK, CANCELLED, EXPIRED), implemented as app.reward_state. Where
this narrative and doc 35 differ, doc 35 governs (doc 82 CROSS-REFERENCE RULE).

CALCULATION INPUTS
Campaign/provider, event type, gross value, user reward rule, fees, bonuses, caps, currency/unit conversion, eligibility, fraud risk, and budget remaining.

SAFEGUARDS
No client-side amount submission can mint money. Duplicate events are idempotent. Reversals create compensating entries. Negative balances are prevented unless an explicit, audited debt/recovery policy is later approved.

EXAMPLE
A provider reports a verified conversion worth a configured net amount. The engine records the provider event, applies the campaign reward rule, creates a pending ledger event, and later makes the reward available only when the configured state transition is satisfied.
