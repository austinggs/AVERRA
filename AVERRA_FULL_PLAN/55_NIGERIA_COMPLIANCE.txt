PURPOSE
Architecture-level compliance checklist for operating Averra in Nigeria. This is not legal advice.

DATA PROTECTION
Design around the Nigeria Data Protection Act/NDPC requirements, including lawful processing, minimization, security, retention, rights handling, processor management, and privacy notices.

CONSUMER PROTECTION
Fees, reward conditions, deposit rules, supported assets, withdrawal rules, service delays, error/recovery policies, and dispute handling must be clear and not misleading.

USER-FUNDED CRYPTO DEPOSITS
Averra must maintain explicit legal/commercial review for any model that receives, records, holds, controls, or applies user crypto to an internal funding balance. The legal structure must distinguish a platform purchase/funding arrangement from regulated deposit-taking, payment processing, custody, money transmission, virtual-asset services, or investment activity as applicable.

REGULATORY REVIEW
The Central Bank of Nigeria oversees the national payments system and maintains licensed Payment Service Provider categories. Current CBN materials emphasize licensing/oversight of payment-system participants.

The SEC regulates applicable capital-market and virtual-asset activities; current SEC materials in 2026 describe VASP participants within its regulatory framework, including the Accelerated Regulatory Incubation Programme. Averra must obtain legal advice on whether any proposed crypto-receiving, custody, brokerage, exchange, or transfer activity falls within applicable SEC requirements.

KYC/AML BOUNDARIES
Formal KYC is not a baseline Averra feature. Manual MiniPay destination verification and admin confirmation of deposits are operational controls, not KYC. This does not prevent legally or provider-required identity, sanctions, AML/CFT, age, fraud, or source-of-funds checks where applicable.

DEPOSIT / REFUND RULES
Unsupported token/network transfers are review cases; blockchain recovery is not guaranteed. Do not promise refunds where technical or legal recovery is impossible. Document complaint handling and decision authority.

NO INVESTMENT REPRESENTATION
User-funded deposits must never be described as investments, stakes, savings products, guaranteed returns, passive-income purchases, or purchases of earning rights.

FEE DISCLOSURE
The 15% Platform Service & Maintenance Fee must be disclosed clearly before eligible withdrawal confirmation, with exact gross/fee/net amounts. It is platform service revenue and not a tax or investment return.

EXTERNAL VERIFICATION
Re-check current CBN, SEC, NDPC, provider, tax, and consumer-protection requirements before production launch and before materially expanding payment or crypto functionality.
