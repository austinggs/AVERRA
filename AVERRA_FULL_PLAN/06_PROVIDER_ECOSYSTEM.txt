PURPOSE
Defines the third-party earning and monetization ecosystem and how providers are managed.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

CANDIDATE PROVIDERS
CPX Research, BitLabs, Lootably, AdGate Media, CPAlead, AdswedMedia, Kiwiwall, Torox/OfferToro, RevU, AyeT Studios, TheoremReach, inBrain, Pollfish, TapResearch, plus future providers subject to approval and eligibility.

PROVIDER CLASSES
Survey, offerwall, CPA, task, rewarded content, advertising, and specialized research providers are modeled by capability rather than by vendor name.

LIFECYCLE
candidate → applied → approved → integration-testing → live → suspended → retired, with rejected as a terminal/non-live outcome.

ELIGIBILITY
Approval, Nigerian traffic availability, incentive policy, webhook/postback support, payment/settlement terms, compliance requirements, and commercial fit must be verified before live use.

ARCHITECTURE
All vendors are behind replaceable adapters; business logic consumes normalized capabilities, statuses, events, and economics.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
This document is a planning/source-of-truth artifact. Concrete library choices, exact endpoint names, provider-specific payload formats, and deployment values may be finalized during implementation only when they preserve the stated invariants and pass the defined verification gates.
