# 01 — PRODUCT AND DESIGN

## Player fantasy
The player is building a simulated economic life, not merely clicking a mining button.

Core loop:
1. Earn virtual money through work/business/trading.
2. Research the world.
3. Deploy capital.
4. React to company and world events.
5. Buy assets.
6. Trade with other players.
7. Grow net worth.
8. Compete in seasons.
9. Unlock achievements and optional premium content.
10. Optionally qualify for tiny promotional real rewards through the separate AVERRA rewards layer.

## Design principles
- Numbers should feel like real-life finance, but remain unmistakably virtual.
- Every major action should have a visible reason and consequence.
- News should provide information, not explicit investment advice.
- High returns must come with meaningful risk.
- Losses should be recoverable; do not hard-lock new players after one mistake.
- The best player is not simply the one with the largest balance; rankings include return, consistency and risk-aware metrics.

## User-facing terminology
Use:
- `Virtual Cash`
- `Virtual NGN`
- `Estimated Earnings`
- `Fictional Coin`
- `Simulated Wallet`
- `Game Market`

Avoid:
- `Guaranteed profit`
- `Cash out your AVR`
- `Real BTC wallet`
- `Investment return`
- `Deposit crypto`
when referring to the simulated systems.

## Accessibility and UX
- Mobile-first responsive layout.
- Dense financial data must have a simplified card view.
- Every destructive action needs confirmation.
- Every financial action gets a transaction reference/history entry.
- User can see why an order failed.
- Charts support readable tooltips and accessible summaries.
