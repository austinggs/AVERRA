PURPOSE
Defines ticketing, evidence, resolution, escalation, financial-support traceability, human-only customer support, and the official Averra contact channels.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

TICKET MODEL
Ticket ID, user, category, priority, status, assignee, timestamps, messages, attachments/evidence, linked entities, and resolution.

LINKED ENTITIES
Task, provider event, survey, offer, referral, game event, reward, ledger entry, withdrawal, payment operation, and transaction reference.

FINANCIAL SUPPORT
Support agents can view required context but must not directly mutate financial balances unless they hold explicit privileged permissions.

ESCALATION
Escalate fraud, KYC, payout, provider, and compliance cases to authorized teams.

SLA
Support priorities and response targets are configured operationally and are separate from payout processing windows.

HUMAN-ONLY CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY
Averra customer support is 100% human-operated. AI-generated customer-support replies are prohibited.

- Support agents MUST be authorized human personnel.
- Averra MUST NOT automatically generate, send, or impersonate support-agent replies using AI.
- Chatbots or conversational AI are not part of the customer-support system.
- Automated system notifications are permitted only for factual state messages such as ticket creation, assignment, status changes, payment status, and closure. Such notifications must not be represented as human-written support replies.
- Financial, payment, fraud, account, and dispute decisions remain under authorized human control.
- Support-agent replies and material actions MUST be audit logged.

OFFICIAL CONTACT CHANNELS
- In-app Support Center / ticketing: primary and authoritative support channel.
- Telegram: @vipaverra, official human-operated contact channel.
- Help Center / FAQ: self-service information channel.
- Support email: not configured yet; do not invent or publish an address.

CHANNEL BOUNDARY
Telegram can receive support requests and payment references, but it does not replace Averra's authenticated ticket and financial records. Support operators must link or create an in-app ticket where a case affects an account, payment, withdrawal, deposit, fraud review, or dispute. Telegram alone MUST NOT authorize a balance mutation, deposit credit, withdrawal approval, refund, or policy exception.

REVIEWS BOUNDARY
Public review/chat content is not a substitute for support. Support agents may link a review to a private support ticket when a user needs account-specific assistance. Support remains 100% human-operated, while review/community content remains subject to the separate moderation system.
