PURPOSE
Unified risk model across accounts, earning, game, referrals, and withdrawals.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

THREAT MODEL
Multi-accounting, fake identities, device farms, VPN/proxy abuse, provider fraud, offer manipulation, survey fraud, referral abuse, gameplay automation, withdrawal fraud, chargebacks, and operator abuse.

RISK SIGNALS
Identity, device, session, velocity, behavioral, provider, financial, game, and destination signals subject to privacy rules.

DECISION MODEL
ALLOW, HOLD, REVIEW, REJECT, RESTRICT, SUSPEND, TERMINATE, with reason codes and evidence.

FINANCIAL INTEGRITY
Risk decisions may hold or reject future events but must not silently rewrite financial history.

APPEALS
Support and admin workflows can review evidence, record outcomes, and link decisions to tickets/audits.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
This document is a planning/source-of-truth artifact. Concrete library choices, exact endpoint names, provider-specific payload formats, and deployment values may be finalized during implementation only when they preserve the stated invariants and pass the defined verification gates.
