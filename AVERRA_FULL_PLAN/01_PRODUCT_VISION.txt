PURPOSE
Defines the long-term product intent, scope, users, value proposition, and strategic boundaries.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

VISION
Build a trustworthy earning platform where users can discover multiple legitimate earning paths, track progress transparently, manage rewards safely, and participate in a durable native game economy without confusing entertainment with money.

PRIMARY PRODUCT PILLARS
Earn through providers, earn through native tasks, play the Mining Game, manage a transparent wallet, refer eligible users, and receive clear operational support.

USER PROMISE
Show users what they are doing, why a reward exists, what state it is in, what conditions apply, and how/when it can be withdrawn. Never present a hypothetical or unfunded reward as guaranteed cash.

BUSINESS BOUNDARIES
No fabricated activity, click fraud, self-completion for personal gain, fake conversions, crypto mining, unbacked balances, or hidden financial mutations.

LONG-TERM EXTENSIBILITY
Provider integrations, native task types, game content, advertiser campaigns, payout providers, and analytics modules must be replaceable or extensible without rewriting the financial core.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
This document is a planning/source-of-truth artifact. Concrete library choices, exact endpoint names, provider-specific payload formats, and deployment values may be finalized during implementation only when they preserve the stated invariants and pass the defined verification gates.
