# 08 — JOBS, WAGES AND LIFE

## Jobs
V1 jobs include:
- Retail Assistant
- Customer Support Agent
- Sales Representative
- Junior Developer
- Accountant
- Graphic Designer
- Nurse
- Teacher
- Engineer
- Product Manager
- Entrepreneur (business route)

Each job has:
- starting salary
- salary growth
- required education
- required skill score
- stress
- working hours
- promotion probability
- satisfaction

## Virtual salary
Salary is virtual NGN and is paid to the virtual cash wallet.
Default starting salaries range from ₦120,000 to ₦600,000 virtual NGN/month depending on job.

## Wage activation
The player may activate an **AVERRA employment/premium participation contract** using the store system.
This changes eligibility for promotional features, not the value of the user's virtual salary.

Default example:
- ₦100 real purchase
- 30 days
- access to eligible promotional reward opportunities

The payment must never be described as buying a guaranteed return.

## Daily life expenses
Basic V1 monthly expense profile can include:
- food
- transport
- housing
- utilities
- communication
- entertainment

Expenses should be configurable by age, city, lifestyle and inflation.

## Progression
Skills improve through:
- education
- work experience
- activities
- achievements

Career choices affect business/trading access but should not prevent trading entirely.

## Life events
Optional later system:
- moving city
- marriage
- children
- inheritance
- emergencies
- major purchases

The economic game must remain fun without requiring every life system from AVERRA's broader simulator.
