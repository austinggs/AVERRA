PURPOSE
Defines moment-to-moment mechanics and progression.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

CORE LOOP
Assign/operate machines, manage energy, collect resources, process outputs, sell/use in virtual economy, upgrade, and unlock new areas.

INTERACTION RULES
Client requests actions; server validates current state, cooldowns, resource ownership, requirements, and action eligibility before committing.

PROGRESSION
XP, levels, unlock trees, missions, achievements, and expansion milestones are separate but composable systems.

FAILURE STATES
Insufficient energy/resources, cooldown, locked area, server rejection, stale client state, and suspicious automation must be handled explicitly.

REWARDS
Game rewards may be non-financial or financial; financial outcomes always pass through the central reward pipeline.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
