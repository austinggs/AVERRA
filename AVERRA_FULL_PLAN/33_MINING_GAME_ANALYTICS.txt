PURPOSE
Defines gameplay telemetry, funnels, performance metrics, and analytical boundaries.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

METRICS
DAU/WAU, session time, zone entry, machine use, resource flow, mission progression, retention, crashes, load time, FPS/frametime, asset load failures, and event participation.

EVENT MODEL
Versioned event names, actor, timestamp, context, game version, session, and relevant non-sensitive dimensions.

SEPARATION
Analytics can explain activity but cannot authorize rewards or replace authoritative game/financial records.

PRIVACY
Avoid unnecessary identifiers; use pseudonymous analytics identifiers where possible.

OPERATIONAL USE
Create dashboards and alerts for performance, economy anomalies, content engagement, and abuse signals.
