PURPOSE
Defines moderation for user-generated, advertiser, support, and game content.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

SCOPE
Usernames/profiles, support content, advertiser creatives, task descriptions, promoted content, and any user-generated game/community surfaces added later.

CONTROLS
Automated detection, manual review, reporting, escalation, blocklist/allowlist, appeal, and audit.

SAFETY BOUNDARY
Fraud/risk enforcement and content moderation are related but distinct decision systems.

ADVERTISERS
Campaign content must be reviewed against applicable platform/provider/advertising policies before publication.

EVIDENCE
Moderation actions retain required evidence and reason codes.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
This document is a planning/source-of-truth artifact. Concrete library choices, exact endpoint names, provider-specific payload formats, and deployment values may be finalized during implementation only when they preserve the stated invariants and pass the defined verification gates.

RELATED DOCUMENTS
See 71_ARCHITECTURAL_LAWS.md, 82_SOURCE_INDEX.md, and the domain-specific database/API/acceptance documents for cross-system contracts.

REVIEWS & COMMUNITY MODERATION
Reviews, comments, and attached images are governed content surfaces. Required actions include reporting, human moderation queue, hide/remove/restore, reason codes, moderator attribution, audit logging, and appeals/reconsideration where appropriate. Deterministic upload/rate-limit checks are allowed. The production system must not use AI to generate public support replies or make final moderation decisions on behalf of human moderators.
