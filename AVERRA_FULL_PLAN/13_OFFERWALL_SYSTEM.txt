PURPOSE
Defines third-party offerwall discovery, tracking, verification, and reward treatment.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

OFFER DISCOVERY
Provider adapter returns normalized offers filtered by country, eligibility, device, category, and provider status.

TRACKING
Averra generates or receives provider-specific tracking links with immutable attribution identifiers.

COMPLETION
Provider callback/postback is authoritative evidence. Browser redirect may be recorded as an activity event but cannot mint funds.

REVERSALS
Provider reversals and chargebacks create compensating financial events and preserve the historical record.

PRESENTATION
Display provider name, completion conditions, estimated reward, pending status, and key restrictions without overstating certainty.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
This document is a planning/source-of-truth artifact. Concrete library choices, exact endpoint names, provider-specific payload formats, and deployment values may be finalized during implementation only when they preserve the stated invariants and pass the defined verification gates.
