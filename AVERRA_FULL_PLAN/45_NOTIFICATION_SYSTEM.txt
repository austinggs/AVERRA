AVERRA — NOTIFICATION SYSTEM

Document: 45_NOTIFICATION_SYSTEM.txt
Version: 1.0
Status: Approved planning baseline
Last reviewed: 2026-09-30

PURPOSE
Defines centralized in-app, email, SMS/push where permitted, and admin notifications.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

EVENTS
Reward pending/available/reversed, task completion, withdrawal changes, Daimo Hurry handling, session/address expiration, payment failure/completion, referrals, security, support updates, and admin alerts.

CHANNELS
In-app notifications are required and must work without any paid third-party notification API. Email/push/SMS are optional future channels according to user settings, legal basis, and operational need.

SUPPORT NOTIFICATIONS
Support notifications are factual database-backed events such as ticket created, assigned, replied to, status changed, or closed. They are not AI-generated support replies. The in-app ticket thread is the authoritative conversational record.

DAIMO OPERATIONS
Hurry request must trigger immediate operational notification to the responsible team. Session creation and impending expiration must be communicated to the user.

RELIABILITY
Notification delivery is not the source of truth. The underlying event/state remains authoritative and notifications are retryable/idempotent.

PRIVACY
Avoid sensitive data in insecure channels; messages use minimal necessary information.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
This document is a planning/source-of-truth artifact. Concrete library choices, exact endpoint names, provider-specific payload formats, and deployment values may be finalized during implementation only when they preserve the stated invariants and pass the defined verification gates.

RELATED DOCUMENTS
See 71_ARCHITECTURAL_LAWS.md, 82_SOURCE_INDEX.md, and the domain-specific database/API/acceptance documents for cross-system contracts.


NO PAID NOTIFICATION DEPENDENCY
The initial support implementation must not depend on Firebase, OneSignal, Twilio, paid email APIs, or another paid notification provider. Use Averra's own notification records and an in-app unread/badge experience. Future notification integrations remain replaceable enhancements.

REVIEW NOTIFICATIONS
When another user replies to a review or configured thread, Averra may create a factual in-app notification. It must identify the event without generating conversational AI text. The review thread remains the authoritative public conversation.
