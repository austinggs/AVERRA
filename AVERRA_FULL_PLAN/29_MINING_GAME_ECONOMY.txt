PURPOSE
Defines the virtual economy, sinks, sources, balance controls, and separation from cash.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

ECONOMY LAYERS
Virtual resource economy, progression economy, and optional promotion-funded reward layer.

SOURCES
Extraction, processing, missions, events, achievements, admin grants, and configured gameplay systems.

SINKS
Upgrades, processing, repairs, expansion, crafting, energy, and other game expenditures.

INFLATION CONTROLS
Production rates, storage, upgrade costs, event multipliers, and sinks are versioned and monitorable.

CASH BOUNDARY
No conversion from virtual resources to withdrawable money exists unless a separately specified, funded Reward Engine program establishes that conversion.
