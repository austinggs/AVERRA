# 21 — COMPETITION AND PROGRESSION

## Seasons
Default season length: 30 days.
Every season starts with:
- ₦1,000,000 virtual cash
- zero market positions
- zero crypto
- season statistics reset

Lifetime account wealth remains available separately from season wealth.

## Leaderboards
Rank by:
1. total return %
2. absolute P&L
3. risk-adjusted return
4. consistency
5. trading volume
6. business profit
7. net worth

Do not make raw wealth the only leaderboard.

## Risk-adjusted score V1
Simplified:
`score = total_return_percent / max(1, max_drawdown_percent)`
Use capped values to prevent division anomalies.

## Progression levels
Player level increases from meaningful actions, not purchased virtual currency.
Level rewards are primarily cosmetic/unlock based.
Do not grant large economic advantages solely for account age or payment.
