PURPOSE
Defines conventional advertising, sponsored placement, rewarded content, and traffic rules.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

INVENTORY TYPES
Standard ads, sponsored placements, rewarded ads/content where permitted, and third-party provider offers.

CRITICAL DISTINCTION
Advertising impression/click revenue is distinct from incentivized offer conversion rewards. Never pay users to click ordinary ads when the ad network prohibits incentivized interaction.

TRAFFIC POLICY
Only legitimate traffic, truthful placement, and provider-compliant behavior. No fabricated impressions, automated clicking, misleading prompts, or forced interactions.

MEASUREMENT
Impressions, clicks, viewability, revenue, fill rate, and provider status are analytical/advertising records, not wallet truth.

MONETIZATION PROVIDERS
Candidate networks include Ezoic, Adsterra, UCD Ads Network, Inlay, Vodra Ads, Buzzer Network, subject to policy/approval and live availability verification.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
This document is a planning/source-of-truth artifact. Concrete library choices, exact endpoint names, provider-specific payload formats, and deployment values may be finalized during implementation only when they preserve the stated invariants and pass the defined verification gates.
