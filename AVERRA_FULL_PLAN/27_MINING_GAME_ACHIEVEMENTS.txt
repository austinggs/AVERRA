PURPOSE
Defines achievement criteria, unlocks, display, and rewards.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

CRITERIA
Achievements subscribe to authoritative gameplay events or derived counters.

UNLOCKING
Unlock is server-side, idempotent, and timestamped.

REWARDS
Badges/XP are game-native. Financial bonuses, if offered, use explicit Reward Engine funding and rules.

HISTORICAL BEHAVIOR
Achievements already earned remain recorded even after criteria definitions change; definitions are versioned.

PRESENTATION
Profiles and leaderboards may display achievement status without exposing sensitive fraud/admin data.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
This document is a planning/source-of-truth artifact. Concrete library choices, exact endpoint names, provider-specific payload formats, and deployment values may be finalized during implementation only when they preserve the stated invariants and pass the defined verification gates.
