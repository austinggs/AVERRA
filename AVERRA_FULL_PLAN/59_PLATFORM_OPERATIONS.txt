PURPOSE
Defines day-to-day operational procedures, ownership, incidents, and runbooks.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

OPERATIONAL DOMAINS
Provider operations, financial operations, payout operations, fraud, support, infrastructure, game operations, campaign operations, privacy/compliance.

DAILY CONTROLS
Provider callback health, settlement/reconciliation, deposit/funding queue, payout queues, Hurry queue, expired Daimo sessions, Cash Link exceptions, fraud backlog, error rates, support backlog, and critical infrastructure health.

MANUAL PAYOUTS
Operator verifies request/destination, performs transfer, records evidence, and marks status only after appropriate confirmation.

DEPOSIT CONFIRMATION
For MiniPay-funded deposits, operator verifies settlement evidence and confirms the deposit only after required payment verification succeeds. Confirmation credits User Funding Balance through the financial service and creates an audit trail.

INCIDENTS
Severity-based response, incident commander, communication, evidence preservation, recovery, and postmortem.

CHANGE WINDOWS
High-risk financial/provider changes require controlled deployment and rollback plans.

This document is a planning/source-of-truth artifact. Concrete library choices, exact endpoint names, provider-specific payload formats, and deployment values may be finalized during implementation only when they preserve the stated invariants and pass the defined verification gates.

SUPPORT OPERATIONS
Support is human-operated only. The Support Center is the authoritative case-management system. @vipaverra is the official Telegram contact channel for human support. Operators should link Telegram conversations to an Averra ticket for traceability.

Initial notification infrastructure must be cost-conscious: first-party in-app notifications are sufficient. Paid notification APIs are not a launch dependency.

Support escalations involving deposits, withdrawals, fraud, disputes, or account changes must follow the corresponding privileged operational workflow; a support conversation cannot bypass those controls.

REVIEWS / COMMUNITY OPERATIONS
Define moderator coverage, report triage, escalation, abuse handling, media retention, and incident procedures for the review/community system. Public negative reviews are not automatically treated as incidents; only defined safety, fraud, legal, or operational triggers require escalation.


## V7 Admin Operations
The Admin Portal is the primary human operational console for deposits, withdrawals, support, moderation, providers, fraud, reconciliation, and system health.
