PURPOSE
Defines rankings, scoring, periods, tie handling, and anti-manipulation.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
The terms MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are used as engineering requirements.

METRICS
Game progression metrics such as XP, production, mission score, or event score; leaderboard metric is explicitly defined.

PERIODS
All-time, seasonal, weekly, daily, or event-specific periods as configured.

AUTHORITY
Scores derive from authoritative game data, not client-submitted totals.

FRAUD
Suspicious accounts can be hidden, frozen, or marked pending through a risk process without rewriting the underlying game ledger/history.

REWARDS
Leaderboard rewards are configured separately; any money reward routes through Reward Engine.
