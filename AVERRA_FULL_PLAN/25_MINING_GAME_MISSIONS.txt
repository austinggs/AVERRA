PURPOSE
Defines missions, objectives, progression, claims, and reward rules.

NORMATIVE LANGUAGE

MISSION MODEL
Mission definition, objective set, progress counters, eligibility, start/end, completion, claim status, and reward reference.

PROGRESS
Server-side events advance mission progress; client displays derived progress.

CLAIMING
Claim is an idempotent authoritative action. A mission cannot pay twice.

REWARDS
Game rewards can be virtual, XP, items, or a financially funded reward. Financial rewards route through the Reward Engine.

EXPIRATION
Expired missions preserve history and do not silently become payable.
